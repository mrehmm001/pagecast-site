Nothing plays
until you've seen it.

The model drafts the slides and the narration. You get them as an editable storyboard, with a live preview of the finished frame beside every field.

Twelve layouts
Bullets, a pull quote, a big number, two columns, full-bleed photo. Change one and the preview redraws as you type.
The page's own pictures
Pick from everything the page had, at the largest size it offers. Nothing is generated, nothing is stock.
Narration you can rewrite
Each slide shows its word count and how long it takes to speak, so you can hit a length exactly.

It plays over
what you were reading.

No tab to hunt for, and no waiting for a video file. It plays on the page it came from: subtitles light up word by word as they're spoken, and you can jump to any moment. Want a file to keep or post? Export it as an MP4.

What happens
when you hit Play.

Your browser does the reading
It reads the page you are already signed in to. A server fetching the same address gets a login wall, a cookie prompt, or a different page altogether.
We do the heavy part
Filtering, the script and the voice run on our machines, every slide narrated at once, so the video starts before the rest is ready. Your browser just draws the slides. Nothing to install, nothing to configure.
What travels, and what doesn’t
The text and pictures from the page you asked about, and nothing else. No other tabs, no browsing history, no background collection.
Honest about the state of it
The hosted version is running for early testers, and what they point it at shapes what it does next.

Get the extension.

PageCast isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so for now it installs by hand. It takes about a minute.

Download the extension

Version 0.4.2 · 487 KB · Chrome or Brave

  1. Unzip it into a folder you'll keep. That folder is the extension, so don't delete it afterwards.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Brave, brave://extensions), turn on Developer mode at the top right, choose Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  3. Pin PageCast from the puzzle-piece menu, open it on any page and choose Sign in.

When there's a new version: download it again, replace the folder's contents, and press the reload arrow on PageCast's card in the extensions page.
