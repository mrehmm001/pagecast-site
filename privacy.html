PageCast

Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

PageCast is a browser extension that turns the page you're reading into a narrated video. To do that, it has to send some of that page to our servers. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who else sees it and how long we keep it. We've tried to keep it short and plain.

Who we are

PageCast is run by Muneeb Rehman, a sole trader trading as Santriq, based in the United Kingdom. In this policy, "we" means Muneeb Rehman trading as Santriq, the data controller for the personal data described here. Our postal address is available on request. You can reach us at pagecast@santriq.com about anything in this policy.

The short version

  • PageCast only reads a page when you ask it to make a video of that page.
  • It sends the text of that page, and the images you keep, to our servers in London. Nothing from your other tabs, and no browsing history.
  • An AI service writes the script. We only use providers that agree not to keep or train on what we send.
  • Pages and working files are deleted after 7 days, and finished videos after 30.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

What we collect and why

Your account

When you sign in, we hold your email address and an account ID. Your password is handled by Amazon Cognito, our sign-in service, and we never see it. We use these to let you sign in, keep your videos tied to you, and contact you about your account.

The pages you turn into videos

When you press Create storyboard, the extension reads the page in your current tab and sends us its text, its title and address, and the images you choose to keep. If you write guidance about what the video should focus on, we receive that too. We use all of this only to make your video. You'll see a notice explaining this, and you have to agree to it, before anything is sent for the first time.

Please don't use PageCast on pages with information you wouldn't want to share, such as online banking, health records or private email.

Your videos

We store the storyboard, the narration audio and any video you export, so you can play and download them.

Usage and plan details

We count how many minutes of narration you use each month and how many videos you start each day, and we record roughly what each video cost us to make. We use this to apply your plan's limits, to stop any one account from running up costs, and to understand how PageCast is used.

Payments

If you upgrade, Stripe handles the payment, through Link, which acts as the seller. Stripe collects your payment details and billing address under its own privacy policy. We never see your card number. Stripe tells us your customer and subscription IDs, which plan you're on, whether the subscription is active and when it renews.

Feedback

If you send us feedback, we keep what you wrote and who it came from so we can act on it and reply.

Server logs

Our servers log requests and errors, which can include your account ID, job IDs and the address of a page you used. We use logs to keep PageCast working and secure.

On your own device

The extension keeps your settings, your consent, and copies of recent storyboards and images in your browser's own storage, so a storyboard survives closing the tab. This stays on your device. Removing the extension removes it.

How long we keep it

  • Pages, images and working files: 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Finished videos and the records of each job: 30 days.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Daily and monthly usage counts: up to about 2 months.
  • Your account, plan and subscription details: while you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove them, apart from anything we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Our legal basis

Under UK data protection law, we use your data to provide the service you've asked for (performing our contract with you). We use usage records, logs and limits because we have a legitimate interest in keeping PageCast secure and affordable to run. We keep payment records because the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. Email pagecast@santriq.com and we'll reply within a month. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Our website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive your IP address when you visit. We don't use analytics or tracking cookies here.

Children

PageCast isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change what the extension sends or where it goes, we'll update this page and ask for your agreement again in the extension before anything else is sent.
